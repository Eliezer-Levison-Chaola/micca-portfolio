Research Projects

SIAPs and Household Dietary Diversity (HDDS)

This study examines the impact of Sustainable Intensification Agricultural Practices (SIAPs) on household dietary diversity in Malawi using IHS6 survey data.

The analysis uses statistical techniques to compare adopters and non-adopters, focusing on how agricultural practices influence nutrition outcomes.

Tools Used: Stata, Excel

Key Focus: Food Security, Nutrition, Agriculture

Food Consumption and Nutrition Analysis

This project evaluates food consumption patterns among households and their relationship with agricultural productivity and income.

It highlights the importance of diversified food access for improving nutrition outcomes in developing economies.

Tools Used: Stata
